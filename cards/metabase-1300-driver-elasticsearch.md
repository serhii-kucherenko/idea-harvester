# Driver: ElasticSearch

source: https://github.com/metabase/metabase/issues/1300
repo: metabase/metabase
+1s: 1506 | comments: 80 | opened: 2015-10-21

## Gap
Teams want Metabase questions/dashboards on Elasticsearch indices the same way they do SQL DBs.

## Why host won't
Open since 2015 with 1500+ reactions; still Type:New Feature with ongoing +1s in 2026 and no official driver.

## Product angle
Maintained commercial Metabase driver (or hosted proxy that exposes ES as SQL/JDBC) with support SLA - community driver exists but is thin relative to demand.

## Competition / workarounds
apinstein/metabase-es-sql-driver (~30 stars). Kibana for ES-native. Opportunity is Metabase-faithful + supported.

## Kill if
Metabase ships an official ES driver, or community driver reaches 'good enough' with active maintainers.

# Google Chat Notification Support

source: https://github.com/getsentry/sentry/issues/49542
repo: getsentry/sentry
+1s: 206 | comments: 34 | opened: 2023-05-22

## Gap
Sentry alerts need to land in Google Chat the way they already do in Slack.

## Why host won't
Sentry staff (sentaur-athena): 'no plan to build the integration' - webhook workaround only. Still Waiting for: Product Owner; users still asking in 2026.

## Product angle
Polished Sentry→Google Chat notifier (issues, regressions, spike alerts) sold as a tiny SaaS or one-click deploy. Beat the email-to-Chat hack.

## Competition / workarounds
jweslley/sentry-googlechat (~20 stars) and a few webhook hacks - none dominant.

## Kill if
Sentry ships native Google Chat, or paid conversion stays near zero after a small waitlist.

# Notion integration

source: https://github.com/getsentry/sentry/issues/69090
repo: getsentry/sentry
+1s: 41 | comments: 11 | opened: 2024-04-17

## Gap
Teams that run work in Notion want Sentry issues as Notion DB rows with two-way updates/comments.

## Why host won't
Sentry staff: 'not in our future roadmap' - pointed at DIY internal integration. Users openly ask for alternatives that already integrate.

## Product angle
Sentry↔Notion sync (create/update pages, status, comments) as a focused integration product.

## Competition / workarounds
bear-in-mind/sentry_notion (~9 stars). Zapier/Make brittle. Room for a reliable dedicated sync.

## Kill if
Sentry or Notion ships first-party, or sync edge-cases kill reliability.
